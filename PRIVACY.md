# Intake Journal Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

You can use Intake Journal on your iPhone without an account. When you use it offline, the app saves your journal on your device and does not send it to the developer. Intake has no advertising or third-party analytics SDKs. The developer does not sell your journal data, use it for advertising, or track you across apps or websites.

## What you record

Your journal can contain medicine or supplement names, amounts, units, times, and anything you write in a note or Daily Journal entry. Intake uses these records to show your history and calculate daily totals. The app stores them on your iPhone until you remove them. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes its local journal; offloading the app keeps its data.

## If you connect a server

Sync is optional. If you enable it in **Settings → Sync**, Intake sends your journal and journal settings to the server you choose so you can use them on other devices. That server receives your account credentials when you sign in, and its operator can access the records stored there. The Intake server stores a password hash. The iPhone app keeps a session token in the device’s Keychain rather than saving your password. HTTPS encrypts the connection; HTTP does not.

Signing out stops sync but leaves existing copies in place. To delete your account and journal from the connected server’s active database, use **Settings → Sync → Delete server account** while the server is online. **Keep a local copy** controls whether the journal stays on this iPhone when you delete the server account. For copies in server backups or help with a server that does not support account deletion, contact its operator.

## Backups and exports

**Back up to iCloud** is on by default. If iCloud Backup is enabled on your iPhone, Apple can include your journal in your device backup. You can turn this off in **Settings → Sync**. To manage existing device backups, use your Apple Account’s iCloud settings.

If you export a journal, the resulting file contains your records and notes. You choose where it goes and who can access it. Deleting an entry or account does not erase files you previously exported.
